## Segmentation Strategies for Better Acquisition

### Strategy 1: Vertical Campaigns
Run separate GHL campaigns per industry vertical:
- **Hospitality Campaign:** Seasonal urgency angles, reputation management, guest experience
- **Developer Campaign:** Project timeline urgency, market intelligence, competitive positioning
- **Healthcare Campaign:** Patient acquisition, practice visibility, compliance efficiency
- **Retail Campaign:** Foot traffic optimization, review generation, local SEO

### Strategy 2: Score-Based Cadences
- **Grade A (score 70+):** Aggressive multi-channel (call + email + LinkedIn) — 3 touches in first week
- **Grade B (score 55-69):** Email-first with call escalation on engagement
- **Grade C (score 40-54):** Monthly nurture with quarterly re-scoring
- **Grade D/F:** Batch enrichment cycles, promote on data improvement

### Strategy 3: Signal-Based Triggers
Use the enrichment data to create trigger-based workflows:
- **Low rating detected** → Auto-assign to reputation management sequence
- **High reviews + high rating** → Pitch premium positioning / case study opportunity
- **No website found** → Offer web presence package
- **Senior title + full data** → Priority call queue within 24 hours

### Strategy 4: Geographic Clustering
- **South Florida cluster** (279 leads): Local events, in-person meetings, community referrals
- **Northeast corridor** (NY 73 + DC area): Remote enterprise engagement
- **West Coast** (CA 83): Tech-forward messaging, digital-first approach

### Strategy 5: Re-Enrichment Cycles
- The 508 Skip/Enrich leads are not dead — they're incomplete
- Schedule quarterly re-enrichment passes
- Each cycle should promote 15-25% of D-grade leads to C or B
- This is recurring revenue for the enrichment service
